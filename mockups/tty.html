what carries over to wayvr 26.8.0

[ok]Square corners and 1px borders: round="0" plus ui_round_multiplier: 0; palette slots map to TTY's ink.

[ok]Text bars: the seek and volume rows are already 20 tappable segments, so each becomes one █ or ░ cell.

[ok]Accent from the wallpaper: the Caelestia sync keeps writing primary / secondary; the neutrals become fixed.

[??]Titles in the border: needs a label with a ground fill placed over the edge. Untested whether wgui lets a child overlap its parent's border.

[??]Block-digit clock: no per-digit cells from _source="clock"; would fall back to a large plain label.

[no]Monospace face: layouts can't pick a typeface, so labels use the system sans-serif unless fontconfig's default is pointed at a mono face.

[no]Typed-in titles, blinking cursors and four-frame redraws: WayVR only exposes ui_animation_speed.
